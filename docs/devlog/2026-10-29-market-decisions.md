# 2026-10-29: Market decisions

- Merged PR #9. The backend side of login is done.
- Joining and leaving (decision 0015): invites by username; leaving freezes your coins, and you can rejoin yourself within 30 days; empty markets are archived and deleted after 30 days.
- Coins (decision 0016): a ledger table plus a stored balance on each membership. Discussed the O(1) and O(n) costs and chose the option that's O(1) for both adding and reading, with history. The database itself prevents debt.
- Allowance (decision 0017): daily, weekly, or monthly with several times or dates, a time zone per market, and delivery by catching up instead of a timer.
- For the shop: whoever posts a product chooses whether its price goes to them or vanishes.
- Started `docs/ideas.md` for future ideas.
- Decided on whole coins only (added to decision 0016).
- Built markets, memberships, and the ledger: create a market, list your markets, change settings, and see your recent transactions. The starting amount is the first ledger entry.
- Tested the "no debt" rule with a real race: two payments of 80 from a balance of 100 at the same moment. One went through, one was refused, and the balance matched the ledger.
- Merged PR #11. Decided invite rules: the inviter picks the expiry, any member can cancel, no duplicates, no limit on re-inviting after a decline (blocking is a future idea), and who invited whom is saved for a future market chat (decision 0015).
- Built invites: send by username, accept, decline, cancel, see pending invites, and the member list. Open invites show only who is invited.
- Merged PR #12. Changed it first: open invites show only who is invited, and who invited each member is saved for a future market chat.
- Closed two loopholes: invites you sent are cancelled when you leave (spotted by me), and coming back never gives the starting amount again (spotted by Claude).
- Built leaving: leave, see markets you left with your frozen coins, rejoin within 30 days, be invited back later, archive when empty, and a once-a-day cleanup that deletes markets empty for 30 days.
- Tested the last two members leaving at the same moment: the market was archived correctly every time.
- Merged PR #13. Decided allowance edge cases: a schedule change starts the new schedule immediately, and time away from a market is never paid (decision 0017).
- Built the allowance: daily, weekly, or monthly schedules with several times or dates, in the market's time zone, paid by catching up whenever a market is opened. Each payout is dated with its scheduled time.
- Caught a gap in the generated migration: Alembic doesn't add new check constraints to existing tables, so I added it by hand.
- Tested 4 people opening a market at the same moment, 10 times: every payout was paid exactly once.
- Markets (4 pull requests) are done on the backend side.
- Next: decide what comes next (the shop, or a first app screen to try it all).
