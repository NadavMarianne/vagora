# Vision

## The idea
A platform for creating small market systems. It starts as a private market for two people with their own currency, and is designed to grow into a platform anyone can configure.

## First use
Two people (the author and his girlfriend) buy things from each other with a shared virtual currency. A purchase is a real obligation: if one buys a favor, the other is expected to do it. How that is acknowledged, enforced, and what happens when it isn't done are open design questions (see open questions).

## Goals
- Works on phones (Android and iPhone), online and available any time.
- Flexible: markets, currencies, items, and earning rules are settings, not code.
- Good enough to become a real product and to serve as a portfolio project.
- Fully documented development process.

## Non-goals
- No real money: no deposits, no withdrawals, no payments. This avoids becoming a payment processor and prevents use for real labor or gambling.
- No microservices. A single backend and a database are enough.

## Open questions
- How is a fulfilled order confirmed, and what happens if it isn't done?
- Frontend technology (web app vs native app) and how it reaches the app stores later.
- Database and hosting choice (free tier for now).
- How earning rules are described in the app.
